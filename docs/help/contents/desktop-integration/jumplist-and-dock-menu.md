## How does it work?

Jumplist menu items are responsible for opening OpenNotes at the specified page. For example:

1. Right click on the OpenNotes icon in your taskbar/dock
2. Click on "New notebook"
3. Notice how the new notebook dialog is opened after the OpenNotes app is focused
